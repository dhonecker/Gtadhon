# Live GTA 6 clipping campaigns (checked 2026-11-20)

I found these through web search. The campaign sites themselves were blocked from this session's network, so every figure
comes from third-party guides and listings. **Open the live brief in Vyro, Content Rewards or Reach before you post.**
Rates change and pools close when their budget runs out.

| Campaign | Host | What you clip | Pay (reported) | Key rules (reported) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MrBeast / MrBeast Gaming** (top pick) | Vyro | MrBeast and MrBeast Gaming videos, incl. *I Survived 100 Hours In GTA 6* | ~$1,000–2,500 per 1M views; most briefs need 5,000 views on a post before it earns | Use #mrbeast and the disclosure tag #mrbeastpartner; must be 18+; clips must be your own and each clip posted once per account; TikTok, Reels, Shorts or X only (no Facebook); no upload button, so you post first and submit the link; the brief may set its own content limits |
| Kai Cenat GTA 6 streams | Content Rewards (Whop) | Kai Cenat's GTA 6 streams | $10,000 pool, paid by views (listing undated) | Brief is behind the join button, so it's unconfirmed |
| LACY GTA VI Marathon | Reach | Lacy's GTA 6 marathon | $12,500 pool, split by share of approved views | Name Lacy in the caption or overlay; use #LACY; TikTok, Reels, Shorts or X; at least 50% of your audience must be in the US, CA, AU, UK or NZ; **no AI-generated content**; no view botting, and one strike bans you from every Reach campaign. Content Rewards reports a 750k-view minimum across your clips, with each clip needing 1k views to count |
| CLIX GTA VI Marathon | Reach | Clix's GTA 6 marathon | $5,000 pool, 400k-view minimum to count | Same rules as LACY, with #Clix |
| Other GTA CPM campaigns | Content Rewards | Varies | $0.70–1.50 per 1k views; $2 minimum payout; caps from $35 to $200 per clip; 10% fee | Usually a watermark from the dashboard kept on screen for the whole clip; no spoilers if the brief says so |

Rockstar runs no paid clipping program. Clipping footage outside a campaign earns nothing and risks copyright claims.
