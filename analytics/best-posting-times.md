# Best TikTok posting times for @gta_6_clips.2 (researched 2026-11-25)

Times in German time (CET). Audience: English captions, so US + Europe.

## Daily slots (priority order)
1. 18:00-20:00: gaming peak (Europe evening + US lunch break). Main slot.
2. 22:00-23:00: Europe late-night gaming.
3. 01:00-03:00: US prime time (19-21 Uhr Ostküste). Good for campaign clips that need US views.
4. Weekend: also 10:00-12:00.

Gap between posts: at least 3-4 hours.

## Days
Best: Tue, Wed, Thu. Fri/Sat good for gaming. Sunday weakest.

## Months
Small month-to-month differences. Plan around events:
- Nov-Jan: strongest phase (GTA 6 launch hype, Christmas gifts, school holidays ~20.12.-06.01.). Post more.
- Feb-Mar: normal level.
- Summer (Jul-Aug): holidays mean more daytime activity, post earlier too.
- Spikes: Rockstar updates, GTA Online/DLC news, trailers. Post within hours.

## Rule
After about 2 weeks, check TikTok Studio > Analytics > Followers > "Follower activity" and replace these benchmarks with the account's own data.
