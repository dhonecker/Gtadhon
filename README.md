# GTA 6 clip page

Working log for a GTA 6 short-form clip page (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels).
Day 1: 2026-11-20 (GTA 6 launch week).

- `campaigns.md`: live paid clipping campaigns and their rules
- `clips/day-01.md`: today's trending sources, chosen moments, clips, rule check and posting plan

## Rules (fixed)

- **Every clip is at least 30 seconds long.** No exceptions; a clip that comes out shorter gets re-cut or combined with another moment from the same creator.
- Only clip creators with an active paid campaign, and only footage their brief allows.
- Hook text burned in for the first seconds; creator name in the overlay where the campaign asks for it.
- No compilation logos or watermarks in the final clip.

## Daily loop

1. Pick a campaign whose creator posted GTA 6 footage today. Only clip footage a campaign pays for.
2. Pull the trending GTA 6 videos and live streams (vidIQ trending + YouTube live search).
3. Read the top source's transcript and mark 3 moments of 20–45 s that need no context.
4. Cut 9:16 clips with burned-in captions and a 1–2 s text hook.
5. Check every clip against the campaign brief (tags, disclosure, length, banned content, spoilers).
6. Post from your own accounts, then paste each post link into the campaign right away. Views count from submission.
7. Log the views after 24 h and 72 h, and keep the hook styles that work.
